# Autonomous Cogntive Development (ACD)

## The Problems with Current AI
- Modern AIs are literally fancy mathematical pavlonian dogs. There is rewards, there is punishment, there are deltas, there are "optimising" functions. But all these are towards 'improvement', and humans don't think in 'improvement'. Humans are spontaneous beings. We get impulse, we do it, and that's what makes us do experiments, thoughts, and being curious.
- Making mere sensory feedback loop doesn't solve the problem, as the problem is engraved in cognition, and not the input.
- We humans don't run in predictive models for ALL cognition. Surely, there is some factor of that, but it is not the only thing.
- Humans are not only based on desires either. A child getting a nutrition through veins still doesn't stop playing with the nearby objects.
- Humans also don't have an engraved definition of an 'object'. We see a glass on a table as a separate entity, but not a cabbage separately from a sandwich. So, defining an 'object' for an AI is also a form of strict dogmatic conditioning.
- We don't think in reward-punishment dualities. A baby pushes a milk carton and it falls. The activity isn't inherently bad. The baby is learning physics, but the milk is wasted. These are 2 different paradiagms, and not a single reward-punishment plane.
- We don't need to add modules such as vision, hearing, e.t.c. Humans don't think in modules.
- Even if we give all sensory feedback, internal state, or whatever, that will make the AI cognitively an animal, and not a human. There is something unique in humans (maybe theory of mind?) that causes rationality to arise, and not just basic correlation.

## What are we really missing?

## Questions that need to be asked.
- If there is no correct answer, no reward, no punishment, and no predefined objective, what causes the AI to produce its next thought/action?
- Let's say we have an internal state which causes the AI to do something, but what does that really do? An internal state may cause spontaneous-ness come in the behaviour, but that doesn't change the cognition. Even if it does change the cognition, how do we adapt the internal state in such a way that it odesn't become a "optimising' function.
- How can the system retain memories and assign them priority without discrete input at start of training?
- How can separate experiences be connected by an AI to come on an conclusion? Logic needs premises, and AI needs to collect appropriate premises.
