# Nano/Micro Bots
- Sub-Millimetre bots for precise functions, whch can be useful in medicine and cleaning oceans. 

## Issues
- Small sub-millimeter robots are not worth the effort. Cramming computer, tools or other material (especially batteries) is very challenging.
- Using Chemical propulsion, or other similar systems is a one time journey. Pills can do the same with appropriate enzyme-sensitive covering.

## Plan
- There will be 100 micrometre metal cylinder. A docking station on upper thigh (allowing drug delivery near uterus, kidneys, intestines, etc)
- We deliver drugs through the docking station into the metal cylinder. Metal cylinder can been propelled to destination using an external magnetic pad (induction).
- The same pad outside the body will have ultrasound to map the blood vessels so that it can create path for cylinder.
- Something radioactive like Carbon 14 can be used to track the cylinder?
- The metal cylinder can be made out of other material so that it is safe even if it is stuck in vessel as it will degrade. 
- This allows long term drug delivery, without need of continuous injections or other invasive methods

## Principles
- All decisions will be made externally, and metal cylinder will open or close to deliver drugs (or maybe capture localised sample of tissues?).
- This avoids complex arrangement and manufacturing of small components, and risks of device failing with heavy metal components (such as batteries).

## Scope
- This has been done in previous researches, but none of them have mitigated the issue of stealth.
- Stealth is necessary to avoid immune-system reactions, protein deposition, calcification of the metal delivery agent.
