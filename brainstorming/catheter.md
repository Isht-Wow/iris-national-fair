# Catheter
- Better monitoring for patients with UTI or other issues.

## Plan
- Make a catheter with a spectroscope, pinger (sends magnetic signal), and receiver (receives magentic signal) at the tip.
- Make it deliver microbots (with LC circuits) which will attach to the bladder lining via 2-3 week biodegradable mechanical pinning (spikes).
- The micrbots will normally attach to the bladder lining's bottom part due to gravity, since there will be no propulsion.
- Microbots will have a spherical shape of 800 micrometres, with roughly 500-600 micrometres for inductor coil and rest for capacitor.
- The capacior dielectric will be a specific resitive chemical which will be sensitive to the parameter (metabolites, pressure, temperature, etc)
- After patient function is restored, catheter will be removed, and microbots will be small enough to pass through urine.

## Principles
- The catheter does chemical analysis of urine, while the microbots analyse the physical properties (temperature, pressure) and certain localised metabolites in the bladder.
