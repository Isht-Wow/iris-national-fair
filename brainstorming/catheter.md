# Catheter
- Better monitoring for patients with UTI or other issues.

## Plan
- Make a catheter. Add a bunch sensors to monitor urine. 
- Make it deliver microbots (with LC circuits) which will attach to the bladder lining. The catheter will have a magnetic coil to control these microbots and collect signals.
- Microbots will collect information about bladder (Pressure, Temperature, etc)
- After patient function is restored, catheter will be removed, and microbots will be small enough to pass through urine.
