# Snake
- Make smooth peristaltic motion-based snake which can be implemented in prosthetic arms and other applications.

## Plan 
- Instead of making a fluid snake like every other lab, we can try some electric or magnetic field sensitive fiber.
- Then we can use a bunch of wires around the muscular body to get continuous peristalsis, rather than discrete contractions and relaxations in fluid chambers.

## Principles
- We can put wires in parallel to fibers, making the design simpler.
- If any localised damage happens to the fiber, and wire is intact, remaining function is preserved, which is advantage over normal robots where a single servo failure or fluid leak may cause robot to get severely damaged.
- Different coiling of same wire in different regions maybe produce unique behaviour of contractions in various parts of robot.
